A small garden,
a shared neighborhood.

A neighborhood in Seoul turned an unused parking area into a community garden. Residents grow herbs and vegetables together. Every Saturday, they share food and exchange gardening tips.

New neighbors say the garden makes it easier to start conversations. Some residents worry about the cost of maintaining the space. Volunteers are discussing how to keep the project open to everyone.
